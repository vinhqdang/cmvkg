\noindent Dear Editor,

\medskip
\noindent I am pleased to submit the manuscript entitled \textbf{``Certified Correction of
Vision--Language Hallucinations: Gains, Preconditions, and a Sequential Failure Mode''} for
consideration for publication in \emph{Neurocomputing}.

\medskip
\noindent The manuscript studies selective prediction for vision--language models (VLMs) under
a distribution-free risk guarantee. Building on a recent impossibility result for predictors
that may only emit or abstain a model's own answer, we introduce Certified-Correction Risk
Control (CCRC): a procedure that may instead substitute the model's answer with one from an
independent evidence channel, while certifying the error rate of everything it emits at a
pre-specified level with a given confidence. We prove validity for the method, characterise its
behaviour, and evaluate it across five benchmarks, two backbones, and a mitigation decoder under
a single, fixed protocol. Beyond the positive results, the manuscript reports the method's
limits in comparable detail: a mixture-level budget bounding self-certified repair, a checkable
precondition for when the method helps, and a sequential extension that we show does not work,
together with a full diagnosis of why.

\medskip
\noindent We believe the manuscript is a good fit for \emph{Neurocomputing}'s scope at the
intersection of learning-theoretic guarantees and practical neural system design: it combines
formal risk-control theory with an extensive empirical evaluation on real vision--language
models, under a protocol designed to be checked rather than taken on faith. All code, extracted
per-item signals, and negative results are released to support independent verification.

\medskip
