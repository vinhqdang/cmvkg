Highlights
==========
(Elsevier submission requirement: 3-5 bullet points, max 85 characters each,
submitted as a separate file from the manuscript PDF.)

- We introduce Certified-Correction Risk Control (CCRC) for VLM answers.
- CCRC accepts, substitutes, or abstains, certifying the emitted error rate.
- A detection-grounded score beats internal-uncertainty scores by 3.1-45.6 points.
- We prove a mixture-level budget bounding how much self-certified repair can gain.
- A generative extension fails: mid-caption repair raises downstream hallucination.
