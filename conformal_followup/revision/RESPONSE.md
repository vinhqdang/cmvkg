## What changed in the conclusions
* Signals were extracted for 1,500 more POPE items (250 new images; POPE-3000 is the whole adversarial split) and 2,500 more AMBER items
  (AMBER-3000, 957 images) on a Colab T4, so there are six cells and twelve (cell, alpha) combinations.
* With the gate fixed on external data at the target's calibration size, the gain over filtering is +0.8 to +7.3 points,
  positive in all twelve, with an image-bootstrap interval excluding zero in seven. It is about one point with 150-200
  calibration items and up to +7.3 with 500 or more.
* The fit-fold gate and the union bound are valid; they help with 500+ calibration items and cost coverage with 150-200.
* Repair accounting depends on the gate: with a small gate under 0.5% of test items change; with a large gate 2.4-5.7% change,
  81-93% of the changes are right, and 4-5% of false positives are corrected.
* The AMBER loss reported before was produced by the start of the sequence (CCRC aborted on 72% of splits under the conventional protocol).
