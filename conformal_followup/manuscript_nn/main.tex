\title{Certified Correction of Vision--Language Hallucinations:\\
Guarantees, Gains and Failure Modes under a Pre-specified Protocol}

\author[bu]{Quang-Vinh Dang\corref{cor1}}
\ead{vinh.dq4@buv.edu.vn}
\cortext[cor1]{Corresponding author. ORCID: 0000-0002-3877-8024}
\address[bu]{British University Vietnam, Hung Yen, Vietnam}

\begin{abstract}
Selective prediction gives a vision--language model (VLM) a distribution-free guarantee on its emitted answers
at the price of abstaining on the rest. When the base error rate $\mu$ exceeds the target $\alpha$, any
predictor that only emits or abstains the model's own answer must abstain on at least $(\mu-\alpha)/(1-\alpha)$ of
inputs. We study a predictor that may emit a different answer. Certified-Correction Risk Control (CCRC) accepts the
model's answer where a correctness score is high, substitutes the answer of a distinct evidence source (an
open-vocabulary detector) where that source is decisive, abstains otherwise, and certifies the error rate of
everything emitted with a fixed-sequence Clopper--Pearson test. We prove validity for policy families indexed by
pre-specified score values under i.i.d.\ sampling of calibration units, and evaluate under a protocol in which the
proved and evaluated procedures coincide: folds are unions of images, and thresholds, repair gate and sequence start
are fixed before calibration. On five
benchmark--backbone cells, certified coverage over filtering rises by $0.8$ to $7.9$ points with an externally fixed
gate, positive in all ten cases, but an image-level bootstrap excludes zero only on the largest cell. Most repaired
items re-emit the model's own answer with detector confirmation; the answer changes for $0.04$--$1.7\%$ of test items
and no false-positive existence error is corrected. The correctness score supplies most of the coverage, and
fixed-sequence testing is fragile with small calibration folds. Repairing a claim mid-caption increases downstream
hallucination, which blocks a fixed-point route to a sequential guarantee.
\end{abstract}

\begin{highlights}
\item A repair-and-certify selective predictor with a valid finite-sample risk guarantee
\item Proved and evaluated procedures coincide: fixed value grid, image-grouped folds
\item Certified gain over filtering is small; bootstrap excludes zero on one cell
\item Most repairs confirm the model's answer; false positives are never corrected
\item Mid-caption repair raises downstream hallucination, blocking a sequential guarantee
\end{highlights}
