Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit the revised version of our manuscript entitled "CMVKG-Guard: Real-Time Hallucination Correction in Vision-Language Models via Dynamic Knowledge Graphs" for consideration as a Research Article in Discover Artificial Intelligence. 

This submission represents Revision 2 (R2) of our paper. In this revised version, we have deeply addressed the outstanding concerns raised by the reviewers and the editor, focusing on enhancing scientific rigor, analytical depth, and honesty. Specifically, we have:

1. Statistical Generalization: Conducted a controlled generalization experiment across three vision-language models (LLaVA-1.5, Qwen-VL, InstructBLIP) with statistical variance (3-run mean ± std) to ensure reproducibility.
2. Robustness and Threshold Cliff Analysis: Added sensitivity evaluations for our detection threshold tau, honestly reporting and discussing the sharp performance cliff observed beyond tau=0.72 due to over-detection.
3. Expanded Failure Case Analysis: Curated test probes to stress-test the system's boundary conditions, provided statistical context on the overall error rate (~11.2% on the full set), and analyzed the practical impact of over-correction.
4. Independent Validation of Reasoning: Evaluated the Layer 3 ReasoningVerifier module in isolation, providing precision/recall metrics and honestly acknowledging the limitations of our lightweight NLI proxy.
5. Honest Contribution Framing: Reworked the Contributions section to transparently position our work as a rigorously evaluated, real-time middleware system and compositional token-level architecture rather than claiming fundamentally new algorithmic components, while drawing clear distinctions from concurrent response-level methods.
6. Detailed Traceability: Provided a comprehensive point-by-point response to reviewers (response_to_reviewers.txt) and a granular, section-by-section change summary (revision2_changes.txt).

We believe this highly transparent, self-critical, and rigorous revision substantially strengthens the manuscript and addresses all remaining concerns. This manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere. We have no conflicts of interest to disclose.

Thank you for your time and continued consideration.
