We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "CMVKG-Guard: Real-Time Hallucination Correction in Vision-Language Models via Dynamic Knowledge Graphs" for consideration as a Research Article in Discover Artificial Intelligence.

Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have achieved remarkable progress, yet their deployment in high-stakes domains is severely constrained by the persistent issue of hallucination. Current mitigation strategies primarily rely solely on extensive retraining (RLHF/DPO) or static retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), both of which face significant limitations in adaptability and real-time performance.

In this work, we propose CMVKG-Guard, a novel training-free framework that addresses these challenges through a paradigm shift: dynamic knowledge grounding. Unlike existing methods, our system constructs a Hierarchical Multimodal Knowledge Graph (DH-MMKG) on-the-fly during inference, allowing it to adapt to unique visual contexts that static knowledge bases often miss. We integrate this with a Cross-Modal Verification Engine (CMVE) that simultaneously validates token generation against visual evidence, factual knowledge, and logical reasoning chains.

Key contributions of our work include:
1. SOTA Performance: Our method achieves 91.5% accuracy on the POPE benchmark and reduces object hallucinations by 84% on the CHAIR dataset, significantly outperforming current state-of-the-art baselines like VCD and mKG-RAG.
2. Real-Time Efficiency: By optimizing for local, dynamic graph construction, we achieve a latency overhead of just 12ms per token, making real-time intervention feasible for the first time in a retrieval-augmented verification system.
3. Unified Approach: We provide the first framework to unify detection and correction in a single end-to-end pipeline without requiring model retraining.

We believe this paper is of significant interest to the readership of Discover Artificial Intelligence as it tackles a critical bottleneck in the reliability of generative AI systems. This manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere. We have no conflicts of interest to disclose.
