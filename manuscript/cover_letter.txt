Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit a revised version of our manuscript entitled "CMVKG-Guard: Real-Time Hallucination Detection and Correction in Vision-Language Models via Dynamic Hierarchical Knowledge Graphs" for continued consideration as a Research Article in Discover Artificial Intelligence.

We sincerely thank the reviewers for their thorough and constructive feedback. The revision addresses all raised concerns substantially and we believe the manuscript is significantly strengthened as a result. Below we summarise the key changes made in response to the reviews.

MAJOR REVISIONS

1. Extended experimental evaluation (6 benchmarks, 8 baselines). The original submission reported results on 2 benchmarks. We now provide a comprehensive cross-benchmark evaluation (Table 3) covering all six datasets (POPE, CHAIR, MMHalBench, MedHallBench, InfoSeek, KVQA) and evaluate against eight baselines, including four recently published methods: OPERA (CVPR 2024), REVERSE (NeurIPS 2025), MARINE (ICML 2025), and DeGF (ICLR 2025). CMVKG-Guard outperforms all baselines across all six benchmarks.

2. Generalisability across VLMs. A new Section 4.4 reports results on InstructBLIP, Qwen-VL, and GPT-4V, demonstrating that CMVKG-Guard operates effectively as a model-agnostic middleware and is not over-fitted to the LLaVA-1.5 backbone.

3. Clarified novelty. The Contributions section now explicitly articulates the four distinct algorithmic advances: (i) online token-level interception (versus offline post-hoc correction), (ii) the per-inference DH-MMKG construction (versus static KGs), (iii) a three-axis non-differentiable UVS (versus single-axis checks), and (iv) step-aware adaptive thresholding to counter hallucination snowballing.

4. Technical clarifications on UVS, CMVE, and decoding.
   - The weight vector w is explicitly confirmed as non-learnable: default values [0.30, 0.40, 0.30] with deterministic, keyword-based query-type re-weighting.
   - CMVE is confirmed as fully non-differentiable (attention thresholding, graph path products, BFS + string similarity).
   - A new Algorithm 2 formalises the LLaVA-1.5 token interception mechanism (VLM.forward() loop with KV-cache append), clarifying how correction integrates into decoding without modifying model weights.

5. Dynamic KG construction details. New paragraphs in Section 3.2.2 specify the incremental O(log k) KG update mechanism, conflict resolution policy (visual evidence weight 1.0 overrides external facts at 0.2), and open-domain scalability strategy (top-5 entity cap + session-level disk caching).

6. Latency measurement protocol. All 12ms latency figures are clarified as end-to-end per-token wall-clock times (VLM forward + CMVE + Look-Ahead Beam Search), measured as median over 500 tokens across 50 POPE samples.

7. Abstract and writing. The abstract is rewritten as a single flowing narrative. Overstated claims (e.g., "robust across all domains") have been moderated in the Introduction and Conclusion. All figure captions have been expanded to be fully self-contained and precisely annotated.

8. Ablation extended. The ablation table (Table 4) now includes two additional rows: "w/o Wikidata (entirely ablated)" and "w/o ConceptNet (Wikidata only)", enabling clean separation of the dynamic construction benefit from the knowledge-base richness benefit.

UNCHANGED CORE CLAIMS
The principal results — 91.5% F1 on POPE, 84% hallucination reduction on CHAIR, and 12ms latency — remain unchanged from the original submission and are fully consistent across the abstract, results tables, and conclusion.

We believe this paper is of significant interest to the readership of Discover Artificial Intelligence as it addresses a critical bottleneck in the reliability of generative AI systems. This manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere. We have no conflicts of interest to disclose.

Thank you for your continued consideration.
