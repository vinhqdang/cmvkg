\noindent\textbf{Manuscript:} ``Real-Time Hallucination Correction in Vision-Language
Models Using Dynamic Knowledge Graph Verification''

\vspace{1em}
\noindent Dear Editor,

Thank you for the quality check of our submission. We have addressed both points
raised, as detailed below. The changes made to the manuscript are also reflected
in the editorial system Declaration step.

\vspace{1em}
\noindent\textbf{Comment 1. Data availability statement}

We have added a Data Availability Statement to the ``Declarations'' section of the
manuscript. Our study relies exclusively on publicly available benchmark datasets
and does not generate any new dataset; the statement therefore identifies these
public datasets, which are cited in the reference list. The statement now reads:

\begin{quote}
``\emph{Data availability:} All datasets analysed during the current study are
publicly available benchmark datasets obtained from their original public
repositories, and are cited in this article: POPE, CHAIR (built on the MS-COCO
dataset), MMHalBench, MedHallBench, InfoSeek, and KVQA. No new datasets were
generated during the current study.''
\end{quote}

\noindent On the editorial system, the data availability declaration has been
updated from ``No'' to ``Yes'', and the statement above has been provided
accordingly.

\vspace{1em}
\noindent\textbf{Comment 2. Dual publication statement}

The dual publication declaration has been updated to ``No'' on the editorial
system. For completeness, we have also added a corresponding statement to the
``Declarations'' section of the manuscript:

\begin{quote}
``\emph{Competing interests:} The authors declare that they have no competing
interests. This manuscript is not a dual publication: it has not been published
previously and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere.''
\end{quote}

\vspace{1em}
\noindent We believe these revisions fully address the quality check requirements
and thank you for your consideration.

The Authors
