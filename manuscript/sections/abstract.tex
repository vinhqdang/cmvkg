\abstract{\revised{Hallucinations in Vision-Language Models (VLMs) remain a critical challenge, often manifesting as object fabrication, factual inaccuracies, or flawed reasoning. Existing mitigation strategies either require expensive retraining or suffer from static knowledge limitations. This study introduces CMVKG-Guard (Cross-Modal Verified Knowledge Graph Guard), a novel training-free framework designed to detect and correct hallucinations in real-time by dynamically constructing and verifying multimodal knowledge graphs during inference.

CMVKG-Guard operates as an end-to-end middleware comprising three core modules: (1) a Dynamic Hierarchical Multimodal Knowledge Graph (DH-MMKG) builder that constructs scene graphs on-the-fly and enriches them with external knowledge; (2) a Cross-Modal Verification Engine (CMVE) that validates token candidates across three layers—visual alignment, knowledge grounding, and logical reasoning; and (3) an Adaptive Confidence Calibration mechanism with Real-Time Correction that dynamically adjusts detection thresholds based on query complexity and domain risk. The framework was evaluated on six standard benchmarks including POPE, CHAIR, and MMHalBench against state-of-the-art methods like VCD and mKG-RAG.

Experimental evaluations demonstrate that CMVKG-Guard achieves a significant reduction in hallucination rates without model retraining. Specifically, the framework targets over 90\% accuracy on the POPE benchmark and an 84\% reduction in object hallucinations on the CHAIR dataset. Furthermore, in knowledge-intensive tasks such as InfoSeek, the system secured an accuracy exceeding 80\%, surpassing baseline RAG methods by over 12 percentage points. Efficiency analysis confirms the viability for real-time applications with a latency overhead of less than 15ms per token.

CMVKG-Guard effectively bridges the gap between perception and reasoning in VLMs through dynamic, multi-layered verification. By providing real-time, explainable corrections without the need for extensive parameter updates, it offers a robust solution for deploying trustworthy multimodal AI in high-stakes domains such as healthcare and autonomous systems.}}
