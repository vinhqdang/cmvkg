   1.2 YOLO ERROR HANDLING IS ONLY QUALITATIVELY ARGUED
   -----------------------------------------------------
   We have moved the YOLO discussion from a single qualitative
   paragraph to a two-axis (mechanism + measurement) treatment.

     - Sec. 4.3 retains the mechanism argument: a spurious visual
       entity acquires near-zero coherent links in K_ext, so its edge

     - Sec. 4.9.3 (KG Noise Robustness) adds the quantitative

       as a proxy for detector-side errors propagated into L_2.
     - Sec. 4.10 (Failure Case Analysis) adds the direct failure-mode
       accounting: missed YOLO recall is named as the proximate cause
       of the dominant residual error (over-correction), with two

     - The Sec. 4.3 paragraph now cites both Sec. 4.9.3 and Sec. 4.10
       inline, so a reader does not have to hunt across the paper.

   1.3 THE REASONING LAYER IS NOT INDEPENDENTLY VALIDATED
   -------------------------------------------------------
   Sec. 4.11 is a new standalone-validation subsection for the
   ReasoningVerifier (S_reason). Seven synthetic probes covering
   multi-hop entailment, temporal contradiction, and negation are run
   in isolation at threshold theta_{L_3} = 0.55. Reported in Table 5:
       Precision = 1.000, Recall = 0.500, F1 = 0.667, Acc = 0.857.

   We do NOT claim this is a benchmark result. The opening sentence of
   the subsection states explicitly that 7 probes is intended as a
   unit-test-style existence proof of the module. The recall = 0.500
   reflects the limitation of the current token-overlap NLI proxy:
   bidirectional temporal cycles like "A before B then after it"
   cannot be detected without explicit negation modelling. Replacing
   the proxy with a dedicated NLI model and re-evaluating on hundreds
   of probes is named as the highest-leverage method-side next step
   in Sec. 4.13 (Limitations, item 3). Reproducible code:
   experiments/layer3_validation.py; JSON at
   experiments/results/layer3_standalone_results.json.

2. HEURISTIC DESIGN WITHOUT ROBUSTNESS ANALYSIS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Sec. 4.9 is a new subsection devoted to sensitivity and robustness
of the heuristic design. The probe set is 500 controlled POPE-style
probes with known ground-truth labels; the synthetic-probe scope is
stated in the first sentence of the subsection so that no
quantitative claim is mistaken for a public-benchmark result.

   2.1 Threshold sensitivity (Sec. 4.9.1, Fig. 6)
       theta_base sweep in [0.50, 0.90]. The system is stable
       (88-100% accuracy) in [0.50, 0.65] and exhibits a SHARP CLIFF
       between theta_base = 0.70 (88.8%) and theta_base = 0.75
       (66.0%). We document this rather than smooth it over, recommend
       theta_base in [0.60, 0.70] as the safe operating range, and
       list the cliff explicitly as item 1 in Sec. 4.13 Limitations.

   2.2 Weight sensitivity (Sec. 4.9.2)
       Six (w_vis, w_kg, w_reason) configurations span 86.4 - 89.8%,
       a 3.4 pp spread. We additionally observe that this flat surface
       implies learned weight adaptation would offer limited headroom
       over the keyword-heuristic re-weighting described in Sec. 3.4 ;
       this is a fairer reading than claiming the defaults are
       optimal.

   2.3 KG noise robustness (Sec. 4.9.3)
       Already summarised under 1.2 above.

   Notation alignment: the threshold variable in Sec. 4.9 is now
   theta_base, matching the calibration formula introduced in Sec. 3.4
   (the earlier `tau' usage was internally inconsistent with methods).

   Reproducibility: experiments/sensitivity_analysis.py and
   experiments/results/sensitivity_results.json.

3. NO FAILURE CASE ANALYSIS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Sec. 4.10 is a new subsection devoted to failure modes. The opening
sentence states that the five probes are intentionally adversarial
and not a random sample, so the 60% case-level failure rate cannot
be misread as an operating-point error rate.

Three-mode taxonomy (each mode traced through the full pipeline):

   - Over-correction (2/2 triggered).
     Visually valid tokens absent from the DH-MMKG receive near-zero
     S_kg and are falsely flagged. Examples: `umbrella' (UVS 0.393),
     action verb `sitting' (UVS 0.394). Proximate cause: YOLO-World
     recall miss.

   - Under-correction (0/2 triggered).
     For probes in which a hallucinated token was spuriously injected
     into the graph, the multi-layer UVS still scored both below
     theta_base = 0.7 (UVS approximately 0.555) and flagged them. We
     interpret this as evidence that L_1 and L_3 act as a partial
     backstop against detection-side contamination, and we explicitly
     caution that 2 probes is not statistically conclusive.

   - Wrong-correction (1/1 triggered).
     A correctly-flagged hallucination is replaced by a contextually
     inappropriate candidate (e.g., `airplane' --> `bicycle'). This is
     an architectural limitation of the current beam search, which
     scores candidates against P_KG and a D-step VLM rollout but not
     against a longer-range semantic-context model. Listed as item 4
     in Sec. 4.13 Limitations; named beam-rerank with sentence
     embeddings is the planned mitigation.

Practical-impact context:
   - 60% case-rate comes from the curated probes.
   - Estimated 11.2% combined error rate on the 500-probe controlled
     set at theta_base = 0.70, with approximately 72% of that mass
     attributable to over-correction.

Reproducibility: experiments/failure_analysis.py and
experiments/results/failure_cases.json.

4. LIMITED EVALUATION TRANSPARENCY
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Two new pieces of transparency machinery have been added so the
reader does not need to consult this letter to understand the scope
of any result:

   (a) Sec. 4.1.2 "Reporting transparency" block.
       Classifies every empirical result in the paper into one of
       three buckets and states which bucket each subsection belongs
       to:

         - Benchmark evaluations (Sec. 4.2-4.4, Sec. 4.7 ablation):
           single-run with fixed seed = 42 on the public datasets in
           Table 1. Single-run reporting matches the standard practice
           of the compared baselines (which also report single-seed
           numbers).
         - Controlled generalization (Sec. 4.5): three seeds * 100
           probes per VLM, full mean +- std.
         - Stress-test and sensitivity probes (Sec. 4.9, 4.10, 4.11):
           controlled synthetic probe sets (500 / 5 / 7 probes),
           single-variable isolation, NOT benchmark numbers.

   (b) Each of the three new analysis subsections (4.9, 4.10, 4.11)
       repeats its bucket and probe count in its opening sentence so
       that scope is local to the subsection.

   (c) Baselines: Sec. 4.1.3 enumerates the four categories of
       baselines (base model, training-free, knowledge-enhanced,
       training-based for reference) and lists OPERA, REVERSE, MARINE,
       and DeGF as the recent additions, each with a citation to the
       original paper. Implementation: official released code with
       default hyperparameters.

   We have NOT artificially re-run the main POPE / CHAIR / MMHal /
   MedHall / InfoSeek / KVQA tables across multiple seeds to inflate
   the variance reporting beyond what was actually measured. The
   single-seed scope of those tables is now disclosed twice (once in
   Sec. 4.1.2 and once in Sec. 4.13 item 6) so the reviewer is aware
   of the limit.

5. NOVELTY REMAINS INCREMENTAL
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

We accept this framing and have aligned the manuscript with it
rather than overstating originality.

   - Sec. 1.3 (Contributions) now positions CMVKG-Guard as a
     rigorously-evaluated SYSTEM contribution and explicitly states
     that the constituent modules --- open-vocabulary detection,
     knowledge-graph retrieval, attention-based visual scoring, and
     beam-search correction --- are individually drawn from the
     existing literature. The novel piece is the composition into a
     per-token, in-the-loop verify-and-correct architecture, together
     with the documented operating envelope (sensitivity cliff,
     failure-mode taxonomy, Layer-3 reasoning limits).

   - The same framing is repeated up-front in Sec. 3 opening and
     again in Sec. 4.12 Discussion, so the reviewer encounters it
     three times: in the introduction, before the methods, and after
     the results. Where MARINE and DeGF address grounding at the
     response or sentence level, CMVKG-Guard is distinguished by
     token-level intervention during decoding (correction before
     error propagation).

   We have made no claim of new algorithms for any constituent layer.

SUMMARY OF WHAT IS STILL OUT OF SCOPE FOR THIS REVISION
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Listed transparently in Sec. 4.13 (Limitations and Future Work) so
the reviewer is not surprised by their absence:

   1. The sensitivity cliff at theta_base > 0.72 is intrinsic to the
      current heuristic threshold and is not smoothed by the existing
      adaptive calibration.
   2. Over-correction remains the dominant residual error, bounded by
      YOLO-World recall.
   3. The Layer-3 NLI proxy is a token-overlap heuristic (recall
      0.500 on the standalone probes); a dedicated NLI model on a
      larger probe set is the planned successor.
   4. Wrong-correction will be mitigated by beam-rerank with a
      sentence-embedding model conditioned on t_{<i}.
   5. The sensitivity / failure / Layer-3 analyses use synthetic
      probe sets (500 / 5 / 7) rather than a public benchmark. Full
      real-benchmark sweeps over the same axes are future work.
   6. The main POPE / CHAIR tables are reported as single-seed,
      consistent with the compared baselines. Multi-seed re-runs of
      those tables are future work.
