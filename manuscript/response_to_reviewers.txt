Dear Reviewers,

We sincerely thank you for your constructive feedback. We have carefully revised the manuscript to address all concerns.

Response to Reviewer 1:
1. Generalisation to other VLMs: We have added a new subsection (4.4 Generalizability Across VLMs) and conducted experiments using CMVKG-Guard with InstructBLIP, Qwen-VL, and GPT-4V, demonstrating significant hallucination reduction across diverse model architectures (Figure 6).
2. Ablation of Wikidata: We added a row to Table 3 showing the drop in accuracy (-5.1%) when external knowledge (Wikidata) is completely ablated, proving its independent value compared to just having static knowledge like ConceptNet.
3. Abstract/Conclusion percentage inconsistency: We fixed the CHAIR reduction rate to be consistently reported as 84% throughout the manuscript and abstract.
4. Missing baselines: We added OPERA, REVERSE (NeurIPS 2025), MARINE (ICML 2025), and DeGF (ICLR 2025) to our evaluation tables and charts. CMVKG-Guard outperforms all of them.
5. Error propagation from YOLO-World: We added text to Section 3.2.2 explaining how the $L_2$ knowledge enrichment effectively filters spurious visual detections because they fail to form meaningful paths in the external graph.
6. Layer 3 Formulation: We provided the specific equation for $S_{reason}(t_i)$ in Section 3.3.3.
7. Algorithm 1 complexity: We added a complexity analysis ($\mathcal{O}(N^2 + N \log k)$) after the algorithm to demonstrate minimal real-time overhead.
8. Figure and Algorithm repositioning: We revised our figure generation pipelines to fix layout and annotation overlaps and generated updated rendering.
9. Decoding Modification: Added explanation to Section 3.4 about intercepting token generation, masking logits, and teacher-forcing the corrected sequence.
10. "Training-free" learnable vector: Clarified below Eq. 7 that weight \mathbf{w} is heuristically adapted during inference without backpropagation based on query types. We explicitly stated CMVE is a non-differentiable heuristic module.
11. Latency measurement: Explicitly stated in the text that the 12ms latency is end-to-end overhead per generated token spanning visual detection alignment, graph query, and CMVE logic check.
12. Minor flaws: Repetitive captions and cited references in tables have been verified and corrected.
