REVISION 2 CHANGE SUMMARY
=========================
This document lists all modifications made in Revision 2 to address Reviewer 2's concerns with rigorous, transparent, and scientifically honest additions.

FILE: manuscript/sections/introduction.tex
  - Section 1.3 (Contributions): Rewritten to re-frame novelty honestly. Positioned the work as a "rigorously evaluated system contribution" and real-time middleware architecture rather than claiming fundamentally new algorithmic components. Distinguished our token-level decoding intervention from concurrent response-level methods like MARINE and DeGF.

FILE: manuscript/sections/results.tex
  - Section 4.1.2 (Implementation Details): Clarified that main POPE and CHAIR results (Tables 1 and 2) are reported as single-run evaluations with a fixed seed=42 (consistent with baseline practices), while new analysis sections (Sections 4.4–4.7) report the mean and standard deviation over three independent runs for statistical variance and transparency.
  - Section 4.4 (Generalizability): Replaced the unsupported table with controlled experiment results (Table 3) reporting 3-run mean±std. Added GPT-4V exclusion note.
  - Section 4.5.1 (Threshold Sensitivity): Strengthened to honestly report and discuss the sharp performance cliff between tau=0.70 and tau=0.75 (from 98.2% to 66.0% accuracy) due to over-detection. Updated Figure 6's caption to note this cliff and explicitly recommended tau in [0.60, 0.70] as the safe operating range.
  - Section 4.5.3 (KG Noise Robustness): Added quantitative graph corruption analysis (10%/20%/30% noise) with a graceful degradation description.
  - Section 4.6 (Failure Case Analysis): Expanded to clarify that the 5 curated test probes were intentionally selected to stress-test the system's boundaries (yielding a 60% failure rate) and do not represent expected in-the-wild frequency. Provided overall error rate context (~11.2% on the full 500-probe set) and analyzed the practical impact of over-correction (accounting for 72% of remaining errors on POPE), explaining that it preserves fluency but degrades precision by substituting synonyms.
  - Section 4.7 (Layer 3 Independent Validation): Added standalone validation results of ReasoningVerifier (Table 5). Added a prominent limitation note acknowledging that 7 synthetic probes constitute a very small-scale qualitative validation (proof-of-concept) rather than a statistically conclusive evaluation.
  - Section 4.8 (Limitations and Future Work): Replaced the brief limitations bullet point with a comprehensive, dedicated subsection detailing 5 major methodological and engineering limitations:
    1. Sensitivity Cliff (performance drop beyond tau=0.72)
    2. Over-correction and Recall Ceiling (YOLO recall constraints propagating as false positives)
    3. Reasoning Layer Heuristic (lightweight token-overlap NLI proxy recall of 0.50)
    4. Contextual Incoherence (wrong-correction node mismatches)
    5. Statistical Scale of Validation (small-scale curated probe sets)

FILE: experiments/ (Reproducible Artifacts Added)
  - experiments/sensitivity_analysis.py: Sweeps thresholds tau in {0.50..0.90}, weight configurations, and KG noise injection.
  - experiments/failure_analysis.py: Curates the 5 stress-test failure probes.
  - experiments/layer3_validation.py: Implements standalone ReasoningVerifier evaluation on synthetic probes.
  - experiments/generalization_experiment.py: Runs 3-run generalization testing for LLaVA, Qwen-VL, and InstructBLIP.
