Highlight macro:

  for visual diffing. Toggle in main.tex preamble. The macro is body-text
  only --- it is intentionally NOT used inside \caption{} or \subsection{}
  to avoid LaTeX fragile-argument errors. Caption- and heading-level
  rewrites are listed in this changelog instead.

-------------------------------------
5. Sec. 3 opening paragraph: added a scope-of-contribution sentence
   that mirrors the introduction's novelty re-framing. The reviewer
   encounters the same framing in both Sec. 1 and Sec. 3 so it cannot
   be missed.
6. Sec. 3.1 problem formulation: removed the contradiction between
   "training-free" and "learning a binary decision function". The
   decision function is now described as designed analytically.
7. Sec. 3.2.2 (L_2 / Knowledge Enrichment): rewritten so the
   conflict-resolution rule is explicit (visual evidence overrides
   external facts only for visually observable attributes; non-visual
   conflicts defer to K_ext), eliminating the prior juxtaposition that

8. Sec. 3.2 - NEW SUBSUBSECTION 3.2.5 "Reasoning Chain Layer (L_3)".
   The "Hierarchical" name in DH-MMKG was previously not honestly
   grounded (only L_1 and L_2 were described). The new subsubsection
   formalises L_3 as a query-specific, lazily-induced layer of
   reasoning chains over L_1 union L_2 and cross-links to Sec. 3.3.3
   for the verification mechanics.
9. Sec. 3.4 (Adaptive Confidence Calibration): every parameter in the
   tau_dyn equation is now defined inline (theta_base, omega_risk,
   omega_complexity, eta, lambda_cap, s_i). The previously-undefined
   lambda was renamed lambda_cap to remove collision with lambda_1,
   lambda_2 used in S_vis.
10. Sec. 3.5 Algorithm 2 (decoding loop): inline "\textbf{break}"
    promoted to its own \State; parallel assignment split into two
    lines so it compiles cleanly under algpseudocode.
11. Notation hygiene (mathematical-rigor fixes, no claim changes):
    - DH-MMKG now correctly expanded as DYNAMIC Hierarchical Multimodal
      Knowledge Graph in two sites that previously dropped "Dynamic".
    - \mathbf{w}^T --> \mathbf{w}^\top  (avoids collision with the
      sequence-length symbol T used in Sec. 3.1).
    - Layer-3 sigmoid written as \mathrm{sigmoid}(...) rather than
      \sigma(...), so the sigma in tau_dyn is not overloaded.
    - Indicator function unified to \mathbb{1} (was inconsistent
      between \mathbb{I} and \mathbb{1}).
    - Layer-2 set membership t_i \in V replaced by an explicit surface-
      form linking function Phi_{tok}: V_{vocab} -> V \cup \{\bot\}.
    - Complexity claim O(N^2 + N log k) corrected to O(N^2 + Nk), with
      the dominant Nk term derived from per-node fact retrieval.
    - LaTeX quote style ("red" --> ``red'').

12. Sec. 4.1.2 (Implementation Details) - NEW "Reporting transparency"
    block. Classifies every empirical result in the paper into one of
    three buckets and states which bucket each subsection belongs to:
       (a) Benchmark evaluations (Sec. 4.2 - 4.4, 4.7 ablation):
           single-run with seed=42 on the public datasets in Table 1.
       (b) Controlled generalization (Sec. 4.5): 3 independent seeds *
           100 standardised POPE-style probes per VLM via the modular
           VLM driver in cmvkg_guard/models/.
       (c) Stress-test and sensitivity probes (Sec. 4.9, 4.10, 4.11):
           controlled synthetic probe sets (500 / 5 / 7), designed to
           isolate one design variable at a time with known labels.
           Explicitly NOT benchmark numbers.
    Reviewer can read the scope of every table without leaving Sec. 4.

13. Sec. 4.3 "On the role of YOLO-World detection errors": reworked
    from a purely qualitative argument into a mechanism + measurement
    answer. The KG-noise robustness numbers (Sec. 4.9.3) are now cited
    inline as the quantitative complement, with explicit failure-mode
    accounting via Sec. 4.10.

14. Sec. 4.5 Generalizability - Table 3 RESTORED with the controlled
    experiment numbers from
    experiments/results/generalization_results.json (3-run mean +- std
    over 100 POPE-style probes per VLM):
        LLaVA-1.5-7B : 14.7 +- 3.4  -->  1.3 +- 0.5  (90.9% reduction)
        Qwen-VL      : 13.3 +- 2.5  -->  1.0 +- 0.0  (92.5%)
        InstructBLIP : 18.0 +- 5.4  -->  2.0 +- 0.0  (88.9%)
    GPT-4V is explicitly excluded from the controlled experiment
    (proprietary API, not seed-controllable). The 1.8% figure for
    GPT-4V cited in the introduction is sourced from the published
    GPT-4V baseline paper and is now framed as a complementary
    indication, not part of the controlled experiment. Caption now
    states "mean +- std, 3 runs" so the unit is unambiguous.
    Reproducible code: experiments/generalization_experiment.py.

15. Sec. 4.7 Ablation Study: added "w/o External Knowledge (Wikidata
    ablated)" and "w/o ConceptNet (Wikidata only)" rows. The
    accompanying paragraph now explicitly decomposes the -5.1% drop
    into a 1.4 pp Wikidata-vs-ConceptNet breadth effect and a 3.7 pp
    dynamic-graph-construction effect, eliminating the earlier
    conflation between "richer KB" and "dynamic graph". The two new
    rows are flagged with $^\star$ in the table caption.

16. Sec. 4.9 Sensitivity and Robustness Analysis - NEW SUBSECTION.
    Scope: 500-probe synthetic POPE-style probe set generated by
    experiments/sensitivity_analysis.py, not the POPE benchmark itself.
    Justified as a single-variable-isolation tool, with full real-
    benchmark sweeps deferred to future work (item 5 in Limitations).
    Three sub-sweeps:
      4.9.1 Threshold sensitivity (theta_base in [0.50, 0.90]):
            stable in [0.50, 0.65]; sharp cliff at theta_base > 0.72
            (98.2% --> 66.0%). Recommended safe range is documented
            rather than papered over.
      4.9.2 Weight sensitivity: six configurations span 86.4 - 89.8%
            (3.4 pp spread). Flat surface implies learned weight
            adaptation would yield limited headroom over the keyword
            heuristic.
      4.9.3 KG noise robustness: random edge-confidence corruption
            (0% - 30% rho); accuracy drops 88.8% --> 80.8%. Framed
            as proxy for detector-side errors and tied back to the
            Sec. 4.3 YOLO discussion.

17. Sec. 4.10 Failure Case Analysis - NEW SUBSECTION.
    Five adversarially-curated probes covering three failure modes:
      - over-correction (2/2 triggered) - dominant residual error;
        root cause is YOLO-World recall miss producing missing graph
        nodes that propagate as L_2 false positives.
      - under-correction (0/2 triggered) - multi-layer UVS acts as
        partial backstop against detection-side contamination;
        cautioned as 2-probe evidence, not conclusive.
      - wrong-correction (1/1 triggered) - corrector lacks longer-
        range semantic context; flagged as architectural limitation.
    Probe set's adversarial curation is stated in the first sentence
    of the subsection so the 60% case-rate is not mistaken for an
    operating-point error rate. Estimated combined error rate on the
    500-probe sensitivity set is 11.2% (= 100% - 88.8%), with ~72% of
    it attributable to over-correction. Reproducible code:
    experiments/failure_analysis.py.

18. Sec. 4.11 Layer 3 Independent Validation - NEW SUBSECTION.
    Seven synthetic probes covering multi-hop entailment, temporal
    contradiction, and negation; threshold theta_{L_3} = 0.55.
    Table 5: Precision 1.000, Recall 0.500, F1 0.667, Accuracy 0.857.
    Subsection states up front that 7 probes is too few for a
    statistical claim and that the result is reported as a unit-test-
    style existence proof. The missed case (bidirectional temporal
    cycle) is attributed explicitly to the token-overlap NLI proxy;
    replacing it with a dedicated NLI model is named as the highest-
    leverage method-side next step (Limitations item 3). Reproducible
    code: experiments/layer3_validation.py.

19. Sec. 4.12 Discussion: each of the three "drivers" of performance
    now points to the subsection that quantifies it (ablation, weight
    sensitivity, sensitivity envelope + failure tail). A new paragraph
    re-positions the contribution as a per-token in-the-loop
    middleware architecture plus its documented operating envelope,
    NOT a claim of new algorithms for the constituent layers. This
    paragraph repeats the novelty framing first introduced in
    Sec. 1.3 and Sec. 3 opening.

20. Sec. 4.13 Limitations and Future Work - NEW SUBSECTION.
    Six items, ordered by impact on headline performance:
       1. Sensitivity cliff at theta_base > 0.72.
       2. Over-correction is the dominant residual error.
       3. Reasoning-layer NLI is a token-overlap proxy (recall 0.500).
       4. Wrong-correction lacks longer-range semantic context.
       5. Statistical scale of new analyses (500 / 5 / 7 / 3*100).
       6. Single-seed reporting for the main POPE / CHAIR tables.
    Items 1-4 are flagged as method limitations and 5-6 as evaluation-
    protocol limitations so the reviewer can distinguish roadmap items
    from paper-level scope items.

21. Manuscript-vs-response-letter language hygiene: removed five

EXPERIMENTAL ARTIFACTS REFERENCED

      -> experiments/results/sensitivity_results.json

alongside the manuscript.
