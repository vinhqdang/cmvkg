Response to Editor's Quality Check Comments

Manuscript: "Real-Time Hallucination Correction in Vision-Language Models
Using Dynamic Knowledge Graph Verification"

Dear Editor,

Thank you for the quality check of our submission. We have addressed both
points raised, as detailed below. Changes made to the manuscript are also
reflected in the editorial system Declaration step.


Comment 1. Data availability statement

We have added a Data Availability Statement to the "Declarations" section of
the manuscript. Our study relies exclusively on publicly available benchmark
datasets and does not generate any new dataset; the statement therefore
identifies these public datasets and they are cited in the reference list. The
statement now reads:

  "Data availability: All datasets analysed during the current study are
  publicly available benchmark datasets obtained from their original public
  repositories, and are cited in this article: POPE, CHAIR (built on the
  MS-COCO dataset), MMHalBench, MedHallBench, InfoSeek, and KVQA. No new
  datasets were generated during the current study."

On the editorial system, the data availability declaration has been updated
from "No" to "Yes", and the statement above has been provided accordingly.


Comment 2. Dual publication statement

The dual publication declaration has been updated to "No" on the editorial
system. For completeness, we have also added a corresponding statement to the
"Declarations" section of the manuscript:

  "Competing interests: The authors declare that they have no competing
  interests. This manuscript is not a dual publication: it has not been
  published previously and is not under consideration for publication
  elsewhere."


We believe these revisions fully address the quality check requirements and
thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
The Authors
